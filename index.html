Privacy Policy for TinyPipes

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MarCas Code ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the smartwatch game TinyPipes.

TinyPipes is an offline puzzle game. It does not require an account, include advertising, or use analytics or tracking services.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through gameplay. The app does not request a name, username, email address, location, contacts, or health information.

The app does not send us gameplay activity, device identifiers, analytics, or crash reports. It does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or advertising SDKs.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

2. Information Stored on Your Watch

TinyPipes saves the following information in the app's private storage on your watch:

  • Completed puzzles and level progress
  • Your current puzzle and pipe rotations
  • Move counts, best move scores, and active gameplay time
  • Your sound preference

This information allows you to resume a puzzle, unlock later levels, view your results, and retain your settings. It is not published or displayed to other players.

3. System Backup and Device Transfer

Depending on your device, account settings, and available platform features, Android or Wear OS may back up your saved progress and sound preference to a system backup service or transfer them to another watch.

These features are handled by the operating system and its backup provider. We do not operate the backup service or have access to your system backups. You can manage available backup and restore options through your device or account settings.

4. Purchases and Third-Party Services

TinyPipes is a one-time paid app with all shipped levels included. It does not contain in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Purchases and downloads are handled by Google Play. The app does not collect or store your payment card details.

Google Play and Android or Wear OS services may process purchase, account, device, or diagnostic information independently of the app. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

5. How We Use Information

Saved game information is used on your watch to provide gameplay, restore progress, record results, and remember your preferences.

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your questions or support requests.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising or profiling.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Saved game information remains on your watch until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Where supported, you can clear the app's storage through your watch's settings. Doing so removes your local progress and preferences.

System backup copies may remain separately and may restore saved information when you reinstall the app. Manage these copies through the relevant device, account, or backup service settings.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

Saved game information is stored in the app's private storage and protected by the security features of your watch's operating system. Security of system backups is managed by the operating system and backup provider.

8. Children's Privacy and Your Choices

TinyPipes does not request personal information from children or other players. It does not include accounts, public profiles, chat, advertising, or tracking.

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, subject to applicable law. We cannot access or delete saved game information stored on your watch or in your system backups; you manage that information through your device or account settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app or our practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact MarCas Code at marcascode@gmail.com.
